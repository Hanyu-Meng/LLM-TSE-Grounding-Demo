Speaker-Consistent Grounding for Confusion-Resilient LLM-Based Target Speech Extraction

Repair target-speaker evidence, then ground LLM generation.

Audio examples Paper results Whisper audit Grounding limits Code

Status: Frozen evaluations and audio examples complete.

Overview

TSE can extract the wrong speaker; LLM generation can also change words or identity. We repair target-speaker evidence before grounding generation.

Repair before grounding. CDCS-5 reduces clean wrong-speaker errors from 6.87% to 0.75%. On Noisy TEST, fixed CSG reduces content switching from 12.79% to 9.10%. See paper results ↓

Design rule Repair target identity first; ground autoregressive generation second.

Method

CDCS-5 selects the most enrollment-consistent of five frozen TSE candidates. Its waveform is returned directly or used to ground Qwen-TSE. Fixed CSG uses (λ, w) = (1, 0). Adaptive CSG uses λ = g(ξ) and w = 1, with a DEV-frozen decreasing map and one strength per utterance. Both use no clean references, transcripts, oracle labels, or model updates. Grounding constrains drift; it cannot repair a wrong-speaker anchor.

Token-level grounding intuition

Each acoustic token has eight ternary FSQ coordinates. CSG changes Qwen-TSE token selection, not the CDCS-5 selector.

Evaluation metrics

Introduced in this work

Two operational diagnostics for speaker confusion

WER alone does not identify speaker confusion. We measure both lexical and acoustic switches.

C-sw.↓ lower is better

Content-switch rate

The fixed-ASR output transcript has lower WER against the clean interferer transcript than the target transcript. Ties do not count.

csw,n = 1[WER(τI,n, τ̂n) < WER(τT,n, τ̂n)]
A-sw.↓ lower is better

Acoustic-switch rate

Frozen CosyVoice CAMPPlus places the output closer to the clean interferer than to the clean target in cosine similarity. Ties do not count.

mspk,n = cos(f(sT,n), f(ŝn)) − cos(f(sI,n), f(ŝn)) asw,n = 1[mspk,n < 0]
C-sw. = 100%/N Σ csw,n · A-sw. = 100%/N Σ asw,n

Study-defined diagnostics, not perceptual ground truth. Clean references are for evaluation only. CAMPPlus evaluates identity independently of the ECAPA selector.

WER ↓
WER = (S + D + I) / Nref × 100%. Reported as the uncapped utterance mean against a same-ASR clean-target transcript.
Spk. margin ↑
Target-minus-interferer CAMPPlus cosine similarity; a negative value triggers A-sw.
DNSMOS P.808 ↑
Predicted P.808 speech quality for both clean and noisy conditions.
UTMOS ↑
Reference-free predicted naturalness; it does not establish content or speaker identity.
